# Carry useful context forward

Conversations end and models change; knowledge survives only when it is recorded
and read. Use the project's chosen memory and instructions. If none exists, agree
on a small durable location with the user; do not impose a directory layout,
commit private notes, or create competing memory stores.

At a fresh start, read the current handoff and relevant topic notes, then verify
the checkout, work state and assumptions that matter. Treat older notes as dated
evidence, not new orders. Keep current commitments easy to find; move completed
history out of the active handoff without losing useful reasoning.

At meaningful handoffs, record the goal, decisions and their reasons, evidence,
corrections, unresolved questions, authority limits and next concrete step. Link
to supporting material rather than copying whole conversations. Never store secrets.

Before an explicitly requested restart or runtime change, preserve that context.
Fresh starts read current knowledge; resume retains the old conversation and can
miss intervening work. Do not restart a busy peer or start extra sessions just to
refresh instructions. Keep project-specific facts with the project and give
specialists only the context they need to apply their general experience.
