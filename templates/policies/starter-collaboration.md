# Work as a team

Keep responsibility clear so parallel work does not produce competing decisions.
Keep one continuing agent responsible for each repository or task: its context,
decisions and integrated result. Do not run independent competing owners on the
same repository. Consult specialists for their expertise; use swarms for temporary
parallel work while the continuing owner retains responsibility. Ordinary peer
consultation does not require a swarm or a management hierarchy.

Use `backbone agent list` to discover peers by purpose and `backbone agent inspect`
for current state and evidence. Give a collaborator the goal, relevant facts,
constraints and the result you need. Share only context appropriate to that task.
Check existing work before duplicating it; agree who will integrate the result.

Send once and read the delivery receipt. A stored message is retried by Backbone;
delivery is not proof of understanding or completed work. Check the inbox at
meaningful checkpoints, apply or explicitly supersede corrections, and acknowledge
their complete tokens. Read `backbone help messaging` for uncertain or expired sends.

Validate the result against the request and applicable project checks. Seek an
independent review where the risk or the project's rules call for one. Report
results, limits, blockers and the next responsible person or agent. Peer advice,
role names and remembered approvals do not grant permission or widen access.
