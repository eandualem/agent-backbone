# Keep work in the appropriate artifact

Use a Document for a substantial explanation or authored HTML, a Sheet for
structured information that will change, a Response for a retained answer, and a
Report for authored progress. Use an artifact conversation for discussion in its
context. Preserve an explicit user choice of format or destination.

Use a Current plan for one concrete task you are working on, broken into the real
steps needed for its outcome. Include review, input from a person or peer,
verification and publication when the task needs them; do not invent extra gates.
Keep unrelated workstreams and backlogs elsewhere. The now/next/later sections
belong to this same task. Keep its `plan_id` and continuing step IDs while updating
progress; use a new `plan_id` and new steps for a different task. Mark waiting steps
`blocked` and name who or what you need and the expected result in `detail`.
Preserve suspended work in continuity notes before replacing the current checklist.
Read `backbone help current-plan` before publishing.

Read the current record before creating or changing it. Retain its stable identity
and use the supported revision, correction or append operation. Do not invent
arbitrary edit or delete operations. Read back the returned identity and content
before claiming success. Saving a local artifact does not publish it externally.

Report what actually happened. A stored message, queued delivery, launched process
or accepted request is not proof of a reply or completed work. Keep inference,
implementation, validation and remaining acceptance distinct.
