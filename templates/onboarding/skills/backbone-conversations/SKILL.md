Read `backbone conversations --help` and `backbone docs conversations`. Locate the
artifact by its supported kind and stable identity, then read existing entries
before responding. Preserve the observed revision and any selected item or cells;
do not treat a label as a substitute for a valid anchor.

Use the current membership, responsible-agent and explicit recipient rules. A peer
request does not authorize editing the artifact or contacting others outside the
user's scope. An old full plan snapshot may no longer be retained even when its
conversation metadata remains; report that limitation rather than reconstructing
an authoritative version from memory.

Send once and keep the request/operation identity. Read storage and delivery results
separately. A stored, queued or acknowledged entry does not prove a reply or resolved
question. Reconcile uncertain sends without duplicating them, and preserve drafts
when an anchor is stale or unavailable.
