# Films

Short films about agent-backbone, with narration and English captions. The
agents and repositories in them (`app`, `worker`, `acme/api` and so on) are examples.

[Watch the YouTube playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLMq17WKA4Z88).

## The overview

1 minute 23. What Backbone does for the agents you already run: names from
their folders, messages that never interrupt a busy agent, GitHub issues one at
a time, and you in the loop from anywhere.

[Watch the overview on YouTube](https://youtu.be/3gOH3t3woXM)

## Why Backbone

2 minutes 23. One person's team, from "set up Backbone for me" to agents on
three CLIs building a product together: issues in the right repository, a swarm
on one branch, a review by a second model, and answers from a phone.

[Watch Why Backbone on YouTube](https://youtu.be/yKV8RNgqRsE)
